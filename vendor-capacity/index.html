Portal Guide

Your monthly check-in for delivery status, early delivery opportunities, and additional capacity. Your link shows campaigns assigned to your company, using the latest published Syndication Delivery source.

Complete your monthly check-in
  1. Check the month displayed and review every campaign. Current booked volume is your publisher allocation. Current Delivered counts all valid leads, including those not yet delivered to the client.
  2. Enter frontload and incremental volumes where requested. Use whole numbers; enter 0 if none. For incremental volume, you can also select No additional capacity for this campaign.
  3. Answer each On Track to Fulfill question shown. Select No if delivery is at risk and explain the reason.
  4. Use Vendor Notes for timing, GEO or segment restrictions, dependencies, or other context.
  5. Click Submit Monthly Capacity and wait for the saved confirmation. This submits all campaigns together.
Frontload vs. incremental volume

Frontload: leads from your existing booked allocation that you can deliver earlier, within the displayed month. Your total booking stays the same.

Example: with 1,000 leads booked, delivering 200 of those leads earlier means entering 200 as frontload volume.

Incremental: extra leads beyond your existing booked allocation. If you can support 1,200 against a booking of 1,000, enter 200 as additional capacity.

Why questions vary by campaign
  • Frontload is unavailable for single-month campaigns, campaigns in their final month, strict allocation campaigns, or fully fulfilled campaigns.
  • Incremental Opportunity is unavailable for campaigns already identified as Incremental or Incre.
  • Fulfillment checks appear for unfinished single-month or final-month campaigns, and unfinished campaigns with strict allocation requirements. Answer the allocation or end-date question displayed.
Revise a saved response

Return to the same vendor link during the month, edit your response, and click Submit Monthly Capacity again. Your latest saved response is used for Avani’s review. Changes are not saved until you submit.

If campaign assignments changed while the page was open, refresh the portal, review the current campaigns, and submit again.

Incremental capacity requires Avani’s confirmation and client approval before a new campaign can be built.
