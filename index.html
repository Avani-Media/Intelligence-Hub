Methodology & Definitions

How the dashboard calculates pacing, quality, forecasts, and operational health.

01

Reporting Snapshot

The snapshot date is inferred from the source by subtracting Time from each active campaign’s end date. The source consistently resolves to one reporting date, which is displayed throughout the dashboard.

02

Campaign Goal & Fulfilment

Booked campaign goal is calculated as Delivered + Leads Remaining. Fulfilment is calculated from delivered volume divided by that goal, with the source fulfilment retained for reconciliation.

03

Current Weekly Velocity

Current weekly velocity equals (Delivered ÷ elapsed calendar days) × 7. Required weekly pace equals (Leads Remaining ÷ days remaining) × 7. The forecast still uses calendar-day timing, expressed as a weekly equivalent for easier planning.

04

Forecast Attainment

Projected delivery at deadline equals current delivered volume plus current daily velocity multiplied by days remaining. Forecast attainment is projected delivery divided by the booked goal.

05

Health Classification

On Track campaigns forecast at least 100% attainment. Watch campaigns forecast 85–99%. At Risk campaigns forecast below 85%. New campaigns with fewer than seven elapsed days and no delivery are labeled Launch Watch.

06

Lead Quality

Valid rate equals Valid Leads ÷ (Valid Leads + Invalid Leads). This indicates the share of processed leads marked valid and should be interpreted alongside each campaign’s validation and suppression requirements.

07

Integration Indicator

The integration metric is a direct count of campaigns where the source field Integrations is TRUE. It is informational and does not alter health or forecast calculations.

08

CSV Refresh

Use Upload CSV for campaign performance and Upload Invalid CSV for lead quality. Each dashboard mode displays only its own EST upload timestamp. Shared cross-device persistence can be connected to a centralized data source when the hosted version is configured.

09

Adjusted Invalid Logic

QA flags are normalized into rejection codes. Codes 1144-1, notsubmitted, and notsubmited remain visible but are excluded from adjusted invalid %. Codes 1001, 1006, and 1013 are removed. Publisher quality status follows the five threshold bands shown in the Invalid Leads view.
